Three Meta models do the work, each where it's best.

Muse Image

Every person

Drew all 47 characters, ages 10 to 90, in up to seven poses each: awake, reaching, dozing. It also turns your own photo into a little Rabble version of you, exactly as you are.

SAM 3.1

Every cutout

Cut every person in the pile out of their background, so they can tumble, stack and lean on each other.

Muse Spark

All the talk

Checks that what you share is kind, then answers it as one of the people beside you, remembering what you said earlier, and picks how the pile reacts with its bodies: a cheer, a lean, a laugh, a doze.

How it plays

A living pile of little people, on a phone, in about a minute.

  1. Become one of them. Optionally, use your photo and you get your own Rabble self. The photo is never saved.
  2. Get caught. You fall into the pile and it catches you. "There you are. We made room."
  3. What's on your mind? Someone beside you asks. Answer, or just sit.
  4. Your words stay yours. What you share goes up as you wrote it. Someone beside you answers, a squeeze reaches you, and the people around you react: a cheer for a small win, a lean on a hard day.
  5. Or just be there. Choose quiet and the pile settles and dozes around you. Drag to look around, pinch to zoom, widen the ring from neighborhood to world.
