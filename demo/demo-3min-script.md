# Rabble: 3-minute demo

**Format:** widescreen 1920×1080. The phone floats alone in the middle of a soft, dark room, and the app is the hero. Large, quiet type appears beside the phone, one idea at a time. There are no slides, bullet lists or busy screens.

**Feel:** like an Apple product film.
- One idea per moment.
- Long, unhurried shots.
- Cuts land on the beat.
- Silence is used on purpose.
- Every claim is shown on the phone as it's said.

**Voice:** a calm voiceover in short sentences, with plenty of space between them. Scenes 1 and 9 have no voice at all.

**Sound:** the app's own sounds sit high in the mix: landings, taps, the little voices, the squeeze, the cheer. Soft whooshes mark scene changes.

## Music

The score is chill, funky, sparse and groovy, built from a few simple parts:

| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| Tempo | 92 BPM, in a minor key with a warm major lift for the ending |
| Drums | A dry, soft kick, and a rimshot on beats 2 and 4. A closed hi-hat with swing comes in only when the groove is "on" |
| Bass | Round and muted, a funky line with lots of rests. Most of the groove lives here |
| Guitar | Clean, short "chank" chords on the offbeat, maybe twice a bar |
| Electric piano | One warm chord now and then. Never a melody that competes with the voice |
| Dynamics | It drops out completely for key moments (the landing, "Just sitting"), then comes back on the downbeat |

Levels through the film:
- **0 · silence**
- **1 · bass and kick only**
- **2 · plus rimshot and electric piano**
- **3 · full groove**, adding hi-hat and guitar
- **4 · ending chord**

## What it takes to make

1. **New footage**, recorded the same way as the promo but in widescreen, with the phone centered:
   - the sky and eagle (scenes 2 and 9);
   - a "Just sitting" visit (scene 8), which needs a second, short recording;
   - a slow turn and close-ups of faces (scene 3).
2. **The three photo steps (scene 4):** I can make the "cut out" in-between picture from the stand-in photo with the same SAM step the app uses.
3. **Music:** a new funky, minimal score written in code, like the earlier ones, cut to these timings so changes land on the beat.
4. **Voice:** the Mac's Samantha voice works but sounds a bit robotic for an Apple-style piece. A real voice would be better: you could record the voice lines on your phone and I'd place them, or we drop the voice and let the on-screen words carry it.
5. **Checks:** no cut shorter than about 3 seconds, normal loudness, and every claim shown on screen as it's said.
